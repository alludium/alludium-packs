Snapshot

Fund fit

State fit against the selected fund mandate, including the fund-specific thesis, stage, geography, cheque and return requirements.

What the company is

Explain the problem, solution, buyer, business model and current traction in plain language.

What the evidence supports

Summarise the strongest supported facts and distinguish company claims from independently corroborated evidence.

Material risk

Name the risk most likely to change the current view.

What is not yet seen

State the most important missing proof without filling gaps with generic prose.

Problem and customer

Solution and value

Technology

Architecture and technical edge

Explain how the product works, what is proprietary, dependencies, defensibility, security and the principal technical proof gap.

Market

Market structure

Cover market size, growth, timing, customer budget and adoption constraints.

Porter's Five Forces

Assess buyer power, supplier power, competitive rivalry, threat of substitutes and threat of new entrants using cited evidence.

Business model

Cover pricing, revenue model, gross margin, retention, expansion, services exposure, scalability and unit-economics evidence.

Go-to-market

Cover ideal customer, sales motion, sales cycle, pipeline, channels, acquisition economics and repeatability.

Competition

Compare direct competitors, incumbents, internal alternatives and substitutes. Explain the wedge, differentiation and durability of advantage.

Team

Cover founder-market fit, role coverage, relevant execution evidence, reference signals, hiring gaps, governance and key-person risk.

Financials

Cover revenue quality, growth, burn, runway, forecast credibility, cash requirements and the metrics still needing verification.

Deal

Cover round size, pre-money and post-money valuation, proposed investment, target ownership, dilution, option pool, lead, other investors, allocation, closing timing, reserves and return sensitivity. Mark unavailable terms plainly.

Open questions

Show only specific unresolved questions that can change conviction, valuation, terms, ownership, approval conditions or next actions. Group by area; use topic and sector as secondary labels.
