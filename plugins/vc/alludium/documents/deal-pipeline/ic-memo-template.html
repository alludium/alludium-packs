IC Memo

Point-in-time decision package, maintained as revisions of one logical Deal memo.

Create or update one standalone safe static HTML artifact using mimeType: text/html. Include the IC round and prior artifact revision; do not record an unconfirmed committee outcome.

Source Inputs

List the Evaluation Report version, underlying company and third-party evidence, model/cap-table sources, term sheet or terms, prior memo revision, and prior confirmed Decision Records used.

Decision ask

Ask: exact action, amount, instrument and ownership.

IC round: date, round label and review purpose.

Recommendation: Proceed / Conditional / Continue work / Pass.

Main tradeoff: strongest bull case versus gating bear case.

Executive summary

Summarize the company, customer problem, why now, confirmed Fund fit, proposed investment, value-creation plan, return path and top risks in one page.
