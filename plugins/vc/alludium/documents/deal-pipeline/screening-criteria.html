Deal Pipeline Screening Criteria

A replaceable firm methodology for deciding whether an opportunity deserves evaluation.

Rendered outputs must be standalone safe static HTML artifacts using a .html filename and mimeType: text/html. The selected Fund thesis is a separate runtime input, not part of this document.

Purpose and boundary

Screening answers one question: is this opportunity worth more investment-team time now? It is not a diligence memo, a legal review, or an IC decision. Apply these criteria alongside exactly one confirmed active Fund thesis. If no Fund is confirmed, assess the company facts but make no Fund-fit conclusion.

Screening decision frame

DimensionQuestionsEarly evidence
ProblemIs the pain material, urgent, and owned by a credible buyer?Founder materials, customer statements, observed workflow.
ProductWhat is the product, does it work, and is the benefit meaningfully better?Demo, usage, proof of concept, customer feedback.
Market and timingIs the entry segment reachable, large enough, growing, and ready now?Bottom-up segment data, adoption signals, regulatory or behaviour shifts.
Business modelAre buyer, pricing, margins, distribution, and sales motion credible?Pricing, pipeline, contracts, sales-cycle evidence.
Fund and financing fitDo stage, sector, geography, cheque, ownership, reserves, and capital path fit the confirmed Fund?Confirmed Fund record, round plan, cap table, use of funds.
Value creationWhat compounds into durable enterprise value?IP, data, workflow position, network, distribution, expertise.
Competition and moatWhy can this company win against direct, incumbent, and substitute options?Customer alternatives, switching costs, defensibility evidence.
Exit and return pathWhat plausible outcome can return the Fund and on what assumptions?Comparable outcomes, buyer logic, dilution and ownership assumptions.
RiskWhat could disqualify the deal, and is the upside large enough for the risk?Contradictions, missing evidence, regulatory or execution exposure.
TeamDo the founders show integrity, insight, ambition, commercial ability, resilience, and capacity to recruit?Track record, references, founder interactions, role coverage.

Evidence and confidence

  • Distinguish founder claims, corroborated facts, inference, and investor judgment.
  • Use Strong / Mixed / Weak / Unknown for each dimension; do not mechanically average.
  • Name conflicts and the evidence that would change the recommendation.
  • Keep gating unknowns to the smallest decision-relevant set.

Decision standard

Proceed when fit and upside are credible and the remaining questions are answerable. Hold when a specific trigger or missing fact should determine timing. Pass when mandate fit, timing, return path, integrity, or another core dimension is materially weak. Every conclusion must include the top reasons and one immediate next action.
