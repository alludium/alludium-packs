Deal Pipeline Evaluation Criteria

Source-neutral evidence-domain method for venture evaluation.

Rendered outputs must use standalone safe static HTML with mimeType: text/html. Apply this methodology with the confirmed Fund thesis; do not copy the Fund thesis into this replaceable firm-methodology document.

Evaluation question

Evaluation asks: what do we currently believe, what remains uncertain, and what work would change the investment view? Treat the report as a living synthesis. Specialist work may be performed at any lifecycle stage and should feed this shared assessment.

Coverage without checklist theatre

  • Do not emit a rote source-checklist narrative. Maintain a compact coverage index by domain.
  • For each material conclusion record evidence, confidence, risk, and next action.
  • Use detailed checks only where risk, missing evidence, or the confirmed Fund methodology makes them decision-relevant.
  • Preserve unanswered or contradicted criteria as explicit gaps rather than filling them with generic prose.

Decision standard

Conclude with the current thesis tension, strongest bull and bear evidence, gating risks, and the smallest work programme likely to change the decision. Separate initial evaluation from formal diligence and legal advice.
