IC Criteria and Guidance

Replaceable firm guidance for preparing and judging an investment decision.

Rendered outputs must use standalone safe static HTML with mimeType: text/html. The IC Memo supports deliberation; only an explicitly confirmed Decision Record records the outcome.

Decision frame

Ask: should the firm invest, on what terms, under which conditions, and why? Make the decision ask precise enough to approve, reject, condition, or send back for specified work.

Minimum IC coverage

AreaCommittee test
Mandate and ownershipExact fit to the confirmed Fund, cheque, ownership, reserves and concentration policy.
Investment thesisThree or fewer underwritable reasons this can become a fund-returning outcome.
Evidence qualityMaterial claims are cited; founder claims, corroboration, inference and judgment are separated.
Market and advantageEntry segment, timing, customer urgency, distribution and defensibility are credible.
TeamIntegrity, ambition, role coverage and ability to execute the next value-inflection plan.
Economics and financingRevenue quality, burn, runway, capital path, valuation, ownership and return sensitivities are explicit.
Risk and downsideGating risks, disconfirming evidence, conditions and stop criteria are visible.
Terms and executionMaterial commercial terms, governance, red lines, diligence status and post-decision work are clear.

Decision options

  • Proceed: approve the ask as stated.
  • Conditional approval: approve subject to named, owned, testable conditions.
  • Continue evaluation: identify the exact missing evidence and next review trigger.
  • Pass: record the durable rationale and any circumstances that would justify reconsideration.
  • Reapproval: state what changed since the prior confirmed decision.

Meeting and revision guidance

Use one logical memo artifact for the Deal and update it for successive IC rounds so artifact revisions remain related. Label each memo version with the review date, round, decision ask and material changes. A distinct financing opportunity should use a distinct Deal or an explicitly new logical memo, not an unrelated duplicate.

Decision standard

The memo must make the bull case, bear case and decision tradeoff legible. It may recommend; it must not claim a committee outcome. Record each confirmed outcome separately in the Decision Record template.
