Term Sheet Review Policy and Firm Red Lines

Replaceable business-review policy. It does not generate term sheets or provide legal advice.

Rendered outputs must be standalone safe static HTML using mimeType: text/html. Firm-specific red lines should replace this default under the same stable document ID.

Review scope

Evaluate the business and investment implications of a supplied term sheet, compare successive versions, and identify counsel questions. Do not draft a term sheet, interpret law, or imply counsel approval.

Version comparison rule

When a prior term sheet is supplied, identify each material addition, deletion and changed value, then assess the business impact and whether it changes the Evaluation Report, IC Memo or prior confirmed Decision Record. Do not mandate a lifecycle move to perform reanalysis or reapproval.

Boundary

Classify findings as business deviation, firm-policy deviation, drafting ambiguity, or legal question. Escalate drafting and legal interpretation to qualified counsel. A policy match is not legal approval.
